Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026. This personal workflow is operated by Luke Anglin.

Information accessed and its purpose

The Google Drive connection accesses file contents and associated metadata needed for personal file storage, archive retrieval, integrity checks, and cleanup. It also reads account storage quota. The video workflow stores generated videos and repair files in its configured private archive.

Google OAuth supplies credentials that let rclone perform these operations. The requested Drive scope permits reading and managing existing files, including uploading, downloading, and deleting files. The automated video archive restricts its storage operations to configured archive paths and recorded objects.

Storage and sharing

Cloud files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. Local copies, upload receipts, and recovery metadata are kept on the owner's computer. OAuth credentials are stored in a local private rclone configuration; they are not included in public pages or video metadata.

The storage connection does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or make Drive files public. Files may be retrieved for the owner's local video workflow, including processing with the rendering or AI services the owner configures. Google user data is not used by this storage connection to train general-purpose AI models.

Retention and deletion

Videos awaiting review and files needed for unfinished repairs remain available. Confirmed uploads become eligible for cloud cleanup after the configured retention period, currently seven days. Active work and the latest Undo actions are protected. Eligible Drive files move to Trash; Google's Trash retention policy applies. The workflow does not automatically empty account Trash.

Local receipts, history, and recovery metadata may remain after media cleanup. The owner can change retention settings, remove local records, or delete cloud files directly.

Control and revocation

The owner can stop the workflow or revoke the connection in their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access prevents further authenticated operations but does not itself delete previously stored files or local records.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
