aip_video_generator

A personal video workflow operated by Luke Anglin. Its Google Drive connection stores private files, retrieves video and repair files, checks available storage, and cleans up eligible archives after upload confirmation.

Google Drive access

The connection uses rclone and Google OAuth to access the owner's Drive account. It requests the Drive permission needed to read existing archives, upload files, and move eligible files to Trash. Connecting this app does not make Drive files public.

The workflow keeps an upload backlog limit and a free-space reserve. New production pauses when those limits are reached. Review, repairs, upload history, and recovery records are managed locally.
